## Open (do these; lowest ID first)

Planning evidence (2026-07-02 session: scratchpad experiments on the real corpus + source-verified research; M9.1's builder re-derives the audio):
- sherpa silero `max_speech_duration` (default 20 s, unset by `make_vad`) is a SOFT cap: past it, threshold 0.5→0.90 + min_silence 0.5→0.1 s, and the cut lands at the next acoustic dip (sherpa-onnx v1.13.2 `voice-activity-detector.cc:51-58`); dip-less speech grows unboundedly. The VAD's CircularBuffer auto-grows — no loss inside the VAD itself.
- Offline decode deletes content wholesale on long segments — but raw stressor numbers carry a construction artifact (codex-review find, re-verified): silero-extent trim alone costs isolated-decode deletions k2v2 24/226 · parakeet 6/226 (onset clipping, D-010; 0.4 s pre-pad recovers to ≈5). 36.5 s continuous concat raw D: k2v2 54/226 · parakeet 24/226 → EXCESS (the honest headline) k2v2 ≈30 (13 %) · parakeet ≈18 (8 %), gap 1.7×. Whole utterances vanish incl. a segment head (first 3.4 s of a 20 s segment). Sweeping max_speech 5–12 s barely helps on dip-less audio (segments stay 9–16 s; raw D≈48). Scratch S/D/I splits were provisional — a tie-biased DP inflated D; M9.1's `cer.py` landed the correct diagonal-preferred tie order (measured excess: k2v2 +17 % at 44.7 s / +28 % at 21.8 s; parakeet +4.6 % / +7.6 %). Lit corroboration, qualitative only (Transformer E2E, concat LibriSpeech; arXiv 2101.05600): WER 3.9→12.6→31 % at ≤20/30/40 s. D-010's "synthetic TTS collapses decode" was this length effect, misattributed (D-010 amended with the codex-review fix).
- RTF stays flat ~0.03 to 36.5 s on the bench box → the live `drop=35` is queue-sizing: `AUDIO_QUEUE_MAX=100` is denominated in chunks of unknown duration (~2.1 s at PipeWire-typical ~21 ms), and `worker` stops draining `audio_q` while awaiting a decode (the decode itself already runs in an executor) — a slow-host stall past the headroom drops mic audio.
- Gap-concat (0.15 s gaps) of CV clips is NOT continuous — clip-edge quiet noise reads as real silence, VAD splits 8/8 cleanly → a valid stressor needs silero-trimmed speech extents + ~10 ms crossfade joins.
